We support owner teams and consultants with focused training on durability analytics, from interpreting inspection data
through to communicating service-life uncertainty.

## Typical training modules

1. Corrosion mechanisms in reinforced concrete and metallic systems
2. How exposure classes shift model assumptions
3. Reading electrochemical indicators and their limits
4. Translating probabilistic outputs into maintenance actions

## Example: simple diffusion model

Below is a simplified expression used to illustrate how transport parameters influence time-to-initiation. We focus on
how uncertainty in inputs expands the service-life envelope rather than the math itself.

```latex
{{</* math */>}}
C(x,t) = C_s \left(1 - \operatorname{erf}\left(\frac{x}{2\sqrt{Dt}}\right)\right)
{{</* /math */>}}
```

If you want a short workshop tailored to your asset class, we can deliver it virtually or on site.
